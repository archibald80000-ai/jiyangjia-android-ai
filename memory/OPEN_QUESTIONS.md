# Open questions

These questions do not block repository bootstrap but must be answered before production claims.

1. Exact Android device model, CPU ABI, resolution, touch and kiosk/auto-start permissions.
2. USB microphone model and whether it includes echo cancellation.
3. Whether audio output is internal, USB, Bluetooth or analog.
4. Development/GPU node hardware and operating location.
5. Store network topology, public IP, firewall and TURN requirements.
6. Final avatar/person/voice ownership and commercial permission.
7. Approved public-facing brand, product, service and compliance content.
8. Production domain, TLS and device-authentication policy.
9. Whether customer recordings/transcripts may be retained and for how long.
10. Target response-latency and acceptable fallback experience after pilot measurement.
